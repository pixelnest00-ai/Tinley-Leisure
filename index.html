Club Med South Africa Beach & Safari

The first Club Med in South Africa, and the first anywhere to pair a beach resort with a dedicated safari lodge as one dual-destination stay. Guests checked in from 4 July 2026, with the official inauguration on 17 September 2026.

The resort

Set on the Dolphin Coast north of Ballito, the beach resort offers four pools, a 500-seat conference room for business events, the first Club Med surf school in the network, and kids' clubs. Design draws on local materials and Zulu craftsmanship, and the build was targeted at BREEAM eco-certification.

The safari lodge

Vikela Safari Lodge sits inside the private Mpilo Game Reserve in northern Zululand and is reserved for Club Med guests, with capacity of roughly 225 guests a night. Stays of one to three nights layer onto a Tinley Manor holiday.

Project timeline

A decade from feasibility to opening

  • 2015
    First feasibility studies with Club Med begin, according to Collins Residential.
  • September 2023
    Contract signed between Club Med and the Collins Residential Consortium, with an investment of more than R2 billion announced under Tinley Leisure (Pty) Ltd.
  • 2024
    Detailed design finalised with Club Med's team and construction starts on the Tinley Manor site.
  • October 2025
    Global reservations open through Club Med's channels.
  • 4 July 2026
    First guests welcomed, following the municipality's occupation certificate.
  • 17 September 2026
    Official inauguration, attended by Tourism Minister Patricia de Lille and Club Med shareholder leadership.
